About me

Diego Bardella

I help companies run private AI, from first idea to production: technical use-case assessment, architecture, GPU sizing and deployment of open-source LLMs and agentic harness, on-premises, hybrid or on EU sovereign clouds.

15+ years in datacenter infrastructure (enterprise private cloud, MSP, Telco NFV), in delivery and presales. I started in AI infrastructure at Dell Technologies, working on early DGX deals. Today I run Dielabs, where I help companies bring private AI to production, and a hands-on inference lab where I benchmark LLM serving and agentic workloads under production-like conditions.

I design, test and deploy systems. AI accelerates the build.

What I do

Technical use-case assessment

From requirements to workload profile, SLOs and costs.

Inference sizing & serving

Workload modeling, capacity planning, SLO-driven GPU sizing. Deploying and tuning open-source models with vLLM, model parallelism and KV cache management.

Benchmark & observability

Repeatable load tests to find bottlenecks and the performance/cost sweet spot. Monitoring TTFT, ITL, throughput, KV cache utilization and GPU saturation.

Agentic AI

Agent harnesses, tool calling and MCP on self-hosted LLMs — and what multi-step, long-context agent traffic does to latency, KV cache reuse and cost per task.

AI applications

RAG over company data, voice assistants and AI gateways, built on the serving stack — from proof of concept to a usable product.

Datacenter & presales

Storage and virtualization (VMware, Nutanix, NetApp). RFI/RFQ, solution design and architecture validation.
