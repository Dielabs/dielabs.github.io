About

The Lab

Dielabs investigates how architectures shape workload behavior in AI systems. Focused on the layers that make inference a service — runtime, serving stack, hardware & fabric — and the system behavior they produce, from latency and throughput dynamics to capacity under load.

I design, benchmark, and validate inference architectures to map performance, trade-offs, and real-world limits — and turn these findings into opinionated frameworks for day 0–1 decisions: sizing, capacity planning, and workload deployment.

Then I help organizations translate these frameworks into production-ready decisions: what to deploy, where, and at what scale, minimizing cost and risk without compromising on performance.
